## Limits

- Training pixels cover the core Nile Delta (30.4–31.5 °N); Upper Egypt and new lands need more tiles (`scripts/gibs_fetch.py`).
- GIBS NDVI is decoded from the official colour map (0.005 bins); checked against ORNL MOD13Q1: mean error 0.002 where pixels align (one-pixel ≈ 240 m location offsets elsewhere).
- NDVI is greenness, not yield or crop type.
- The physics inputs use a generic root zone (100 mm) and a linear NDVI→Kc rule (assumptions, editable in backend/pulse.py).
